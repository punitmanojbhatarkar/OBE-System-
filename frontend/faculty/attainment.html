NBA SAR CRITERION 3

Why Attainment % ≠ Student Marks Obtained (Official NBA Formula)

In Outcome-Based Education (OBE), Attainment % is NOT the average percentage marks scored. It is the percentage of students in the cohort who meet or exceed the target passing threshold (e.g., ≥ 60%). Even if students score 65%, 70%, or 80% (not full marks), if 100% of the class achieves the 60% threshold, the Cohort Attainment is mathematically 100% (NBA Level 3).

Attainment % = (Students scoring ≥ Target Threshold ÷ Total Students Appeared) × 100
