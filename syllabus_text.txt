COURSE ABSTRACT:
This course introduces core concepts of data preprocessing, statistical analysis, regression, and time
series forecasting for data-driven decision-making. It covers data wrangling techniques, descriptive and
inferential statistics, hypothesis testing, regression models, correlation analysis, and time series methods
like ARIMA and Prophet. Real-world case studies from retail, healthcare, real estate, and stock markets
are used to build analytical and predictive modeling skills.
THEORY COURSE CONTENTS
UNIT 1 Linear Algebra and Matrix Computations 06 HOURS
Applications/Case Study: Principal Component Analysis (PCA), Neural Network weight transfor-
mations
Contents: Vectors, vector spaces, Scalars, vectors, matrices, tensors, Matrix operations: addition,
multiplication, inverse, Rank, null space, orthogonality, Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, Singular Value
Decomposition (SVD).
Self Study: Gaussian Elimination
Further Readings: Matrix Decomposition
UNIT 2 Probability and Random Variables 08 HOURS
Applications/Case Study: Covid Data Analysis or similar
Contents: Probability definitions: Classical, empirical, axiomatic, Conditional probability, Bayes’
Theorem, Discrete and continuous random variables, Common distributions: Bernoulli, Binomial,
Poisson, Normal, Exponential, Expectation, variance, standard deviation, Covariance and correlation
Self Study: variance, standard deviation Common distributions: Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson,
Normal, Exponential
Further Readings: multivariate Central Limit theorem
UNIT 3 Statistics, Sampling, and Visualization 08 HOURS
Applications/Case Study: Covid Data Analysis or similar
Contents: Descriptive statistics: Measures of central tendency and dispersion, Skewness and kurtosis,
Outlier detection, data transformation, Normalization and standardization, Sampling techniques:
Simple random, stratified, cluster, systematic, Central Limit Theorem, Sample size calculation, margin
of error, Sampling techniques and bias, Confidence intervals, point estimation, Hypothesis testing:
Z-test, T-test, Chi-square, ANOVA, F test, Type I II errors, p-values, A/B testing and interpretation.
Self Study: Data Visualization, F test, T-test
Further Readings: A/B testing and interpretation

Contents: Simple and multiple linear regression, Bias-variance tradeoff, Underfitting vs. Overfitting,

Self Study: Accuracy, Precision,Recall,F1-Score, ROC-AUC, Regression: RMSE,MAE, R2,
Further Readings: Evaluation metrics for ML/DL Model
UNIT 5 Calculus and Its Role in Deep Learning 08 HOURS
Applications/Case Study:
