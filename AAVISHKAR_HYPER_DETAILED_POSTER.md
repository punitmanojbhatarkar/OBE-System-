## 🟨 THE EXHAUSTIVE FEATURE SET (CONTRIBUTION - 20 MARKS)

*(This section details exactly what we built under the hood. It proves extreme technical depth).*

### 1. Governance & Curriculum Engineering
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Secure, isolated portals for Admin, HOD, Faculty, and Students.
- **Course Champion Module:** Senior faculty structurally define syllabus units, reference materials, and lock the master blueprint.
- **Granular CO Definition:** Mapping Course Outcomes to specific Bloom's Taxonomy Levels (L1-Remember to L6-Create) and Assessment Methods.
- **Multi-dimensional Matrix Mapping:** Strict 1-2-3 weighted correlations between COs, POs (1-12), and PSOs (1-3).
- **Pre-semester Gap Analysis:** Identification and mapping of curriculum gaps against industry requirements.

### 2. Multi-Agent AI & Assessment Workflows
- **Python FastAPI Backend:** High-performance routing leveraging LangChain/multi-agent architecture.
- **AI Curriculum Agent:** Uses NLP to validate whether faculty-designed question papers match declared Bloom's levels, rejecting misaligned assessments.
- **AI Evaluator Agent:** Performs automated, unbiased grading of subjective student responses against dynamic, Champion-defined rubrics.
- **AI Insights Agent:** Analyzes department-wide attainment trends and drafts pedagogical action plans for HODs.
- **Unified Marks Grid:** High-speed data entry interface for IA (Internal), MSE (Mid-Semester), ESE (End-Semester), and Assignments.

### 3. The Deterministic Mathematical Engine
- **Dynamic Thresholding:** Calculates direct attainment using complex percentile logic (e.g., Target: 60% of students must score >60% on CO-mapped questions to achieve Level 1; 70% for Level 2; 80% for Level 3).
- **Assessment Weighting:** Aggregates marks across varied assessments based on custom CO-weightage distributions.
- **Indirect Attainment Engine:** Ingests, normalizes, and scales 5-point Likert data from Student Course Exit Surveys.
- **Formula Execution:** Final CO Attainment = (80% Direct Attainment) + (20% Indirect Attainment).
- **Matrix Multiplication:** Automatically propagates final CO levels through the CO-PO/PSO mapping matrices to generate absolute Program Attainment scores.

### 4. CQI Analytics & NBA Compliance
- **Mathematical Gap Analysis:** Computes the exact numerical delta between targeted CQI levels and actual attained levels for every outcome.
- **Remedial Action Tracking:** Forces faculty accountability by tracking underperforming COs and demanding structured action plans.
- **HOD Command Center:** Real-time graphical analytics powered by Chart.js.
- **Multi-variate Radar Charts:** Visualizing PO and PSO attainment health across entire departments.
- **Course-wise Bar Charts:** Comparing average CO health to identify weak faculty or tough subjects.
- **1-Click NBA Export:** Instant generation of perfectly formatted Excel spreadsheets specifically designed for NBA SAR (Self Assessment Report) Table 3.1.2.
- **Audit Logs:** Immutable tracking for accreditation inspectors.
